Our Menu:

Varrious mouth-watering local dishes for your selection
We have:

  • Waakye: A traditional Ghanaian dish made with rice and beans, served with a variety of toppings such as fried fish, boiled eggs, and shito sauce.

  • Jollof Rice: A popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, and spices, often served with chicken or fish.

  • Plain Rice: Simple yet delicious, our plain rice is cooked to perfection and can be paired with a variety of sides.

  • Salads: Fresh and healthy salads made with a variety of ingredients such as lettuce, tomatoes, cucumbers, and your choice of protein.
